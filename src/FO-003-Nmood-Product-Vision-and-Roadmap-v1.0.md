## 1. Product Vision

Build the world's most trusted AI-powered platform for real-world human connection — where people spend less time on screens and more time living.

## 2. Product Mission

Use AI to recommend the right people, experiences, and communities to each member, so that meaningful relationships form in the real world — and protect the trust that makes those relationships possible.

## 3. Product Principles

Inherited from FO-001, Section 4, and made operational:

1. **Human First** — every feature begins with a human outcome.
2. **Trust before Growth** — no feature ships if it erodes trust.
3. **Safety before Engagement** — reduce engagement before reducing safety.
4. **Privacy by Design** — collect the minimum, protect the maximum.
5. **AI assists humans — not replaces them** (FO-004).
6. **Real-world experiences over endless scrolling.**
7. **Quality over quantity.**

## 4. North Star Metric

**Meaningful Real-World Connections (MRWC):** the number of relationships formed through Nmood that result in at least one completed in-person experience, sustained over time.

MRWC is the single metric that aligns product, growth, and AI. It cannot be gamed by screen time, signups, or vanity engagement. Every feature is evaluated against its expected contribution to MRWC.

Supporting metrics (defined in Section 13) provide context, but MRWC is the North Star.

## 5. Release Philosophy

Every release must improve at least one of: Security, Performance, Accessibility, User Experience, Trust, Safety, Reliability (FO-001, Section 14). A release that improves none is not shipped. A release that degrades any is not shipped until corrected.

Releases are:

- **Small and safe** over large and risky.
- **Member-tested** before Founder-approved.
- **Localized** in all supported languages before general availability (FO-001, Section 4).
- **Audited** for AI, safety, and privacy impact (FO-004).

## 6. Release 1.0

**Theme: Trust Foundation.**

Release 1.0 establishes the trusted core of Nmood. It is certified production-ready per the PB-004 Final Certification.

**Includes:**

- Authentication, onboarding, and profile with trust verification.
- Home modular widget engine (HM-UX-001).
- Discovery, search, and AI concierge.
- Experiences: create, join, chat, day-of, ratings.
- Circles: create, chat, members, memories.
- Messaging and notifications with cross-tab and cross-device sync.
- Calendar with conflict detection and reminders.
- Safety center: report, block, guidelines, emergency.
- Membership tiers (Explorer / Premium) with server-verified premium.
- Full localization across 7 languages including Arabic RTL.
- Founder Mission Control: 25 modular operating pages.
- Security infrastructure: role-based access, audit logging, hard-delete governance.
- Performance: 106 lazy-loaded routes, React Query caching, global error handling.
- Accessibility: ARIA, semantic HTML, focus states, alt text.

**Success criteria:** certified across all 11 sections of PB-004.

## 7. Release 1.1

**Theme: Depth & Inclusion.**

Release 1.1 deepens trust and broadens inclusion without expanding scope.

**Planned:**

- Full Arabic translation of all keys (from 175 to 2,870).
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility audit and remediation.
- Additional languages based on market expansion (FO-006).
- Enhanced AI concierge with explainable recommendations (FO-004).
- Trust Score transparency for members (FO-007).
- Improved notification cross-device latency.
- Performance budget enforcement via CI.
- Store readiness submission (App Store / Google Play).

## 8. Release 2.0

**Theme: The Platform.**

Release 2.0 opens Nmood as a platform for trusted third-party communities and experiences, under strict governance.

**Planned:**

- Verified partner communities with brand partnerships (FO-006).
- University and corporate programs (FO-006).
- Ambassador program integration (FO-006).
- Advanced AI memory and personalization under FO-004 governance.
- Expanded international markets (FO-006).
- Open API for trusted partners, governed by FO-007 and FO-004.

## 9. 3-Year Vision

Within three years, Nmood is the leading AI-powered human connection platform in its launch markets, measured by MRWC and trust score — not by downloads. Nmood is profitable in its primary market, localized in 12+ languages, and recognized publicly for safety and privacy leadership.

## 10. 5-Year Vision

Within five years, Nmood is one of the world's most trusted platforms for genuine human connection, operating across multiple continents and cultures without compromising the mission. The brand is associated with real relationships, not screen time. AI at Nmood is a reference for responsible, explainable, human-centered design (FO-004).

## 11. Product Evolution Strategy

Nmood evolves through three durable motions:

1. **Deepen trust** — better verification, clearer transparency, stronger safety.
2. **Deepen connection** — better matching, richer experiences, longer relationships.
3. **Expand access** — more languages, more markets, more inclusive design.

Nmood does **not** evolve by adding engagement loops, follower mechanics, or content virality. Those paths are explicitly refused (FO-001, Section 6).

## 12. Innovation Framework

Innovation at Nmood is governed by the Decision Framework (FO-001, Section 13) and the AI Constitution (FO-004).

- Every innovation must answer: "Does this help people build meaningful human relationships?"
- AI innovations must pass the AI Release Policy (FO-004).
- Safety-critical innovations require Founder sign-off.
- No innovation ships that weakens a Permanent Rule (FO-001, Section 18).

## 13. Success Metrics

**Primary:**
- Meaningful Real-World Connections (MRWC) — North Star.

**Trust & Safety:**
- Trust Score distribution.
- Safety incident rate and time-to-resolve.
- Report resolution quality.

**Engagement (context only):**
- Experiences completed.
- Active circles and communities.
- Retention at 30 / 90 / 365 days.

**Quality:**
- Member satisfaction.
- Crash-free sessions.
- Accessibility audit pass rate.
- Localization completeness (FO-001, Section 4).

**Avoided as primary metrics:**
- Screen time.
- Daily active users as an end in itself.
- Vanity engagement.
