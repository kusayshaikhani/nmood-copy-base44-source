## Purpose

To define the permanent brand of Nmood so that every expression — visual, written, spoken, automated — is consistent, trustworthy, and human. The brand is not decoration; it is the public form of the mission defined in FO-001.

## Scope

This document governs all Nmood-branded output: product UI, marketing, social media, customer support, press, partnerships, advertising, and AI-generated communication. It applies globally and across all supported languages.

## 1. Brand Vision

To be the most trusted brand in human connection — known not for attention, but for the real relationships it helps create.

## 2. Brand Promise

Nmood promises to help you meet the right people, in the real world, around things you genuinely care about — and to protect your trust while doing it.

## 3. Brand Values

The brand expresses the values in FO-001: Trust, Respect, Integrity, Inclusivity, Human Connection, Safety, Transparency, Accountability, Innovation, and Simplicity. Brand decisions that conflict with these values are rejected.

## 4. Brand Personality

Nmood is **warm, calm, confident, and human.**

- Warm — never cold, corporate, or transactional.
- Calm — never loud, urgent, or sensational.
- Confident — never boastful or desperate.
- Human — never robotic or formulaic.

Nmood speaks like a trusted friend who is genuinely happy you are here — not a salesperson.

## 5. Tone of Voice

**Clear. Kind. Honest. Optimistic. Grounded.**

- Clear: short sentences, plain words, one idea at a time.
- Kind: assume the best in people.
- Honest: never over-promise; say what is true.
- Optimistic: believe people can connect well.
- Grounded: no hype, no hyperbole, no urgency tricks.

Tone may shift slightly by channel, but the character never does.

## 6. Writing Guidelines

- Write for humans, not algorithms.
- Use second person ("you"), not third person ("users").
- Use active voice.
- Prefer short sentences. Break long ones.
- One thought per sentence.
- No jargon. If a technical term is necessary, explain it once.
- No exclamation marks unless genuinely joyful.
- No emoji unless in social or support contexts where warmth is the point.
- Translate all copy through the Nmood Localization Service (FO-001, Section 4). Hardcoded strings are prohibited.

## 7. Copywriting Standards

- **Headlines:** benefit-led, never fear-led.
- **Subheads:** clarify, never repeat.
- **Body:** specific over abstract. Real examples over adjectives.
- **Calls to action:** verb-first, low pressure ("Find your circle", not "Sign up now!").
- **Errors:** written as a person apologizing, not a system excusing.
- **Empty states:** encouraging, never shaming.
- **Confirmation:** affirm the action and the human.

## 8. Vocabulary

- **Member** — never "user" in member-facing copy.
- **Circle** — a trusted group around a shared interest.
- **Experience** — a real-world activity.
- **Pal** — a connection made through Nmood.
- **Host** — a member who organizes an experience.
- **Community** — a trusted collective.
- **Trust Score** — evidence-based reliability measure.
- **Mission Control** — the Founder and admin operations interface.

## 9. Words We Never Use

- "Users" (in member-facing copy) — we say "members."
- "Match" / "Matches" — we are not a dating app (FO-001, Section 6).
- "Swipe" — we do not reduce people to a gesture.
- "Viral" / "Go viral" — we are not social media.
- "Followers" — we are not a follower economy.
- "Engagement bait" / "hook" / "dopamine."
- "Addictive."
- "Growth hack" (in member-facing copy).
- Any language that objectifies, ranks, or commodifies people.

## 10. Words We Always Prefer

- "Member," "people," "someone," "you."
- "Connect," "meet," "gather," "belong."
- "Trust," "safe," "respected," "welcome."
- "Real-world," "in person," "together."
- "Help," "support," "guide."

## 11. Messaging Framework

**Audience → Problem → Promise → Proof → Invitation.**

1. **Audience:** Speak to a person who wants real connection but is tired of performative platforms.
2. **Problem:** Screens have replaced relationships.
3. **Promise:** Nmood helps you meet the right people, in real life, around what you love.
4. **Proof:** Real experiences, trusted communities, verified members.
5. **Invitation:** Find your circle.

## 12. Emotional Positioning

Nmood positions itself emotionally as **relief, not excitement.**

- Relief that someone finally built a platform that wants you off your phone.
- Relief that you can be yourself without performing.
- Relief that trust, not virality, is the currency.

We sell calm confidence, not FOMO.

## 13. Logo Usage

- The Nmood wordmark is the primary logo. It must remain legible and unaltered.
- Minimum clear space equals the height of the wordmark's lowercase "o."
- Never recolor, stretch, rotate, or animate the logo in ways that reduce legibility.
- Never place the logo on busy, low-contrast, or off-brand backgrounds.
- The brand name "Nmood" is never translated or transliterated (FO-001).

## 14. Iconography

- Icons are line-based, rounded, minimal, and consistent in stroke weight.
- Icons illustrate function, never decoration.
- No emoji as system icons.
- All icons support RTL/LTR logical alignment (FO-001, Section 4).

## 15. Color Psychology

Nmood's palette expresses calm trust and warmth.

- **Primary (purple):** dignity, calm, creativity. Used for trust signals and primary actions.
- **Accent (soft lavender):** warmth, welcome, human.
- **Success (green):** affirmation, completion.
- **Warning (amber):** caution, never alarm.
- **Destructive (red):** reserved for genuine danger.
- **Neutrals:** quiet, readable, never stark.

Color is a semantic system, not a decoration. It must remain consistent across light and dark themes and across all localized markets.

## 16. Typography

- **Primary typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans — modern, friendly, legible.
- **Headings:** confident, well-spaced, never shouty.
- **Body:** readable, generous line height.
- **Multilingual:** typeface must support all seven supported languages, including Arabic (RTL) and Cyrillic.
- Never use more than two weights in a single view.

## 17. Photography Style

- **Real people, real moments.** Candid over posed.
- Natural light over studio.
- Diverse subjects, reflecting every market Nmood serves.
- People looking at each other, not at the camera, where possible.
- No stock-photo clichés of isolated individuals staring at phones.

## 18. Illustration Style

- Soft, rounded, human.
- Used to explain, not to entertain.
- Inclusive representation by default.
- Consistent line weight and palette with the rest of the brand.

## 19. Motion & Animation Principles

- Motion is purposeful. It guides attention, explains change, or affirms action.
- Never decorative, never attention-seeking, never looping to bait engagement.
- Animations are short, gentle, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Motion never simulates urgency to drive a tap.

## 20. Social Media Voice

- Conversational, warm, brief.
- Share real member moments (with consent).
- Never trend-chase. Never engagement-bait.
- Respond to members as a person, not a brand.
- No follower-count celebration; growth is a quiet byproduct of trust.

## 21. AI Voice

AI-generated communication on behalf of Nmood follows FO-004 and this Bible.

- Identifies itself as AI where a member could reasonably be confused.
- Calm, helpful, transparent.
- Never manipulative, never urgent, never emotional persuasion.
- Escalates to a human promptly (FO-004, AI Escalation Rules).

## 22. Customer Support Voice

- Empathetic first, accurate second, fast third.
- Own the problem; do not blame the member.
- Plain language; no internal acronyms.
- Say sorry when sorry is owed.
- Close with a clear next step.

## 23. PR Voice

- Factual, understated, confident.
- Lead with the mission and the member.
- Never speculate. Never over-claim.
- Transparency over spin (FO-001, Core Values).

## 24. Press Guidelines

- All press inquiries route through the Founder or designated spokesperson.
- No off-record comments that contradict this Bible or FO-001.
- No commentary on competitors.
- Member data is never disclosed to press without legal basis and consent.
- Statements are localized where relevant, but the source message is singular.

## 25. Brand Governance

- This Bible may only be revised by Founder approval, recorded in the Version History.
- Any brand deviation requires Founder sign-off.
- Brand consistency is audited across product, marketing, support, and AI output.
- The brand is the public form of the mission in FO-001. To weaken the brand is to weaken the mission.
