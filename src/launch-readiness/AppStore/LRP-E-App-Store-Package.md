### Data Safety Guidance (Google Play Data Safety section)

**Data collected:**
- **Personal info:** Name, email, photo (for your profile) — optional.
- **Contacts:** Only when you choose to invite a pal; we don't upload your address book.
- **Location:** Approximate, to show nearby experiences and circles — optional, off by default.
- **Activity in app:** Your experiences, circles, and messages — used to improve your recommendations.
- **Diagnostics:** Crash and performance data to improve stability.

**Data handling:**
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- You can request deletion of your data at any time.
- We never sell your data.
- We never share your data for advertising.

**Security practices:**
- Verified profiles
- Human-reviewed moderation
- Security event monitoring and audit logging
