## 1. Pricing Strategy

**Free tier — Explorer:**
- Join experiences, connect in circles, basic AI recommendations.
- No ads. No data selling. No engagement limits designed to frustrate.

**Premium — subscription:**
- Monthly and annual plans.
- Deeper AI matching, unlimited experiences, advanced insights, priority access.
- Annual priced at ~10× monthly (two months free) to reward commitment.

**Pricing principles (FO-001 §12, FO-006):**
- Transparent. No hidden tiers. No surprise charges.
- Cancellation is one tap, no retention pressure.
- Free tier is genuinely useful — Premium is a deeper version of the same product, not a paywall around the mission.
- Local pricing per market where purchasing power differs (PPP-aware).

**Pricing guidance (USD reference, localize per market):**
- Monthly: $9.99
- Annual: $79.99
- Founder/grant path exists for ambassadors, support, and partners (FO-001 §6) — no payment, audit-logged.

## 2. Revenue Forecast

**Model assumptions:**
- Closed Beta: 0 paid (free by design).
- Public launch city: seed members → small paid conversion.
- Premium conversion benchmark (trust-first product): 3–6% of active members.

**Year 1 (illustrative, single launch city):**
- Active members by month 12: ~5,000–8,000
- Premium conversion: ~4%
- ARPU blended: ~$3–$4/month per active member
- Year 1 revenue: modest; goal is trust foundation, not revenue.

**Year 2–3:**
- Add 2–4 cities per year (FO-006 §8).
- Conversion improves with trust depth.
- Corporate + university programs add a partner-revenue line.
- Path to profitability in the primary market by Year 3 (FO-003 §9).

**Rules:** Forecasts are labeled expectations, never promises (FO-010 §11). Unit economics and trust metrics accompany every revenue number.

## 3. Growth Forecast

**Growth engine (FO-006):** Trust → referrals → great experiences → word of mouth → community.

**Beta:** ~100–300 testers (Section J).

**Year 1:** Single city, density-first. MRWC growing WoW is the only growth metric that matters.

**Year 2:** 2–3 cities. Ambassador program scales. University program live in 2 campuses.

**Year 3:** 5–8 cities. Corporate program live. 12+ languages. Profitable in primary market.

**Year 5:** Multi-region, multi-continent (FO-003 §10).

**Anti-metrics (not forecast):** viral coefficient, DAU spikes, paid acquisition dominance.

## 9. Executive Summary

**Nmood** is an AI-powered Human Connection Platform on a mission to help people build meaningful real-world relationships — and to use technology to encourage less screen time, not more.

We are the opposite of social media: no follower economy, no virality, no engagement tricks. We use AI to connect people around shared interests, trusted communities, and real experiences, then get out of the way. We are free to join, verified, human-moderated, and privacy-by-design. Premium unlocks deeper matching with no ads and no data selling.

We measure success in decades: meaningful relationships created, experiences completed, healthy communities, and durable trust (FO-001 §16–17). We grow through trust and referrals — never through manipulation (FO-001 §12).

We are entering Closed Beta with a production-certified platform (PB-004), a complete Founder Documentation Suite (FO-001 through FO-010), and a launch readiness program that treats trust as the only durable currency (this document).
