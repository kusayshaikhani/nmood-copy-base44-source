## 1. Launch Campaign

**Theme:** "Less screen time. More life."

**Objective:** Establish Nmood as the trusted, real-world alternative to social media in the launch city.

**Core message:** Technology should help you live more, not scroll more. Nmood uses AI to get you into the room with the right people — and then gets out of the way.

**Channels:**
- Local PR and earned media (press release below)
- Founder-led content (LinkedIn, a personal essay on why Nmood exists)
- Ambassador-led city activation (Section 5)
- Referral program seeded to first 100 members (Section 2)
- Paid: calm, honest social ads — "Meet real people, in real life." No urgency, no FOMO.

**KPIs:** MRWC (North Star), referrals per member, experience completion rate, press mentions, waitlist-to-signup conversion.

**Budget principle:** Spend on experiences and trust, not on attention-buying.

---

## 2. Referral Campaign

**Name:** "Bring a pal."

**Mechanic:** After a member completes a real experience, they can invite one pal. Both get a trust boost and a limited Premium trial.

**Rules (FO-006 §3):**
- Invite only after a positive experience.
- No cash bounties. No spam prompts.
- Quality over quantity — a member who invites people they'd genuinely vouch for.
- Never pressure invites to people the member doesn't recommend.

**Copy:** "Had a great time? Bring a pal. They get Premium for a week. You get the credit."

**KPIs:** Referral-driven signup share, referral-to-experience completion, member Net Promoter.

---

## 3. Ambassador Program

**Purpose:** Trusted local members who represent Nmood in a city or campus (FO-006 §5).

**Profile:** Warm, trusted, active in the community. Selected for values, not following.

**Responsibilities:**
- Host a regular Nmood experience (monthly minimum)
- Welcome new members personally
- Model community standards (FO-001 §8)
- Feed city-level insights to the team

**Compensation:** Fair, transparent, not performance-gamed. Fixed monthly retainer plus experience budget.

**Governance:** Ambassadors are members first. They follow the same standards and are subject to the same enforcement (FO-007).

---

## 4. University Program

**Purpose:** Help students build real social connections on campus (FO-006 §6).

**Approach:**
- Partner with student organizations, not just administration.
- Campus ambassadors host weekly experiences.
- Privacy-first: no data shared with the institution.
- Measure belonging, not installs.

**Copy:** "College is better when it's real. Nmood helps you meet people on campus who actually get you."

**KPIs:** MRWC within student population, student retention, ambassador-led experience count.

---

## 5. Corporate Program

**Purpose:** Help employees connect in real life (FO-006 §7).

**Approach:**
- Offer Nmood to employees as a wellbeing benefit.
- No employer access to employee member data — ever.
- Employees keep full control of their Nmood identity.
- Partnership terms reviewed against FO-001 Permanent Rules.

**Pitch:** "Give your team a reason to connect that isn't a Slack channel. Nmood helps employees meet in real life, around shared interests — with full privacy."

**KPIs:** Partner count, employee activation rate, MRWC within partner orgs.

---

## 6. Influencer Kit

**Who we work with:** Creators known for genuine, calm, real-life content — not engagement-bait or lifestyle-flex creators. Alignment with FO-002 is mandatory.

**The offer:**
- Early Premium access
- Experience budget to host real meetups for their community
- Co-branded experience (not co-branded hype)
- No scripted "go viral" briefs — we ask for honest, real content

**Guardrails:**
- Disclose the partnership (FTC / local equivalent).
- No "swipe" or "match" language. No dating framing.
- No urgency or FOMO mechanics.
- Content reviewed for brand consistency before publish.

## 12. Email Campaign

**Sequence (5 emails, to waitlist + early members):**

1. **Welcome** (Day 0) — "You're in. Here's what Nmood is." + how to join first experience.
2. **The why** (Day 2) — Founder essay excerpt. "We built the opposite of social media."
3. **How it works** (Day 4) — AI recommendations, circles, experiences. 3 steps to your first meetup.
4. **Safety first** (Day 7) — Verified, moderated, privacy by design. Your trust is the product.
5. **Your first experience** (Day 10) — Personalized invitation to an experience this week.

**Rules (FO-002):**
- Subject lines: warm, honest, no clickbait.
- One idea per email.
- Unsubscribe in every email.
- Localized per member's language.
