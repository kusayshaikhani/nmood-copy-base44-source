We are not a dating app. We are not social media. We are a place where real relationships begin around shared interests, trusted communities, and experiences you actually attend.

## 3. AI

**Headline:** AI that helps you meet — not keep you tapping.  
**Body:**  
Nmood's AI recommends people, experiences, and communities that fit your life. It explains its suggestions, never manipulates your attention, and always lets a human decide.

- **Explainable.** You see why a recommendation was made.
- **Controllable.** You adjust, reset, or turn off personalization.
- **Human-overseen.** A person reviews high-impact decisions.
- **Private.** AI uses only what you consent to share.

**CTA:** Read the AI Constitution

**SEO Title:** Nmood AI — Responsible, Explainable, Human-First Recommendations  
**SEO Description:** Nmood's AI helps you meet the right people in real life — responsibly. Explainable recommendations, human oversight, and privacy by design. Read our AI Constitution.
