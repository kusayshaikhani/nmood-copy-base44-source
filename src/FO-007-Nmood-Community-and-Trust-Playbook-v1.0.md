## 1. Community Vision

Every Nmood community is a trusted group where members can be themselves, belong, and connect in the real world. Communities are built around shared interest, location, or identity — never around status, virality, or performance.

## 2. Community Standards

Inherited from FO-001, Section 8.

**Respect for:**
- All nationalities, cultures, religions, languages.
- Human dignity and human rights.

**Zero tolerance for:**
- Hate, harassment, racism, discrimination, violence, extremism, fraud, exploitation.

These standards apply equally to every member, host, ambassador, partner, and employee.

## 3. Trust Philosophy

- Trust is earned through evidence, not claimed through words.
- Trust is dynamic — it rises with positive behavior and falls with harm.
- Trust outweighs engagement (FO-001, Section 9).
- Trust cannot be bought, sold, or transferred.

## 4. Moderation Principles

- **Consistent.** Rules apply equally regardless of member status.
- **Transparent.** Members understand what was enforced and why.
- **Proactive.** Detect harm before it escalates where possible.
- **Human-led.** AI assists (FO-004); humans decide enforcement.
- **Fair.** Moderators are trained, supported, and protected.

## 5. Enforcement Framework

Enforcement is proportionate to harm and history.

| Severity | Example | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Low | Minor incivility | Warning + education |
| Medium | Repeated or targeted harassment | Restriction + review |
| High | Hate, violence, exploitation | Removal + ban |
| Critical | Threat to life, illegal activity | Immediate ban + legal escalation (FO-008) |

Every enforcement action is logged in an immutable audit record.

## 6. Appeals Process

- Every enforcement action is appealable.
- Appeals are reviewed by a human not involved in the original decision.
- Appeals are resolved within a defined, published timeframe.
- A successful appeal reverses the action and restores the member's standing.
- Appeal outcomes are recorded for audit.

## 7. Trust Score Governance

- The Trust Score is evidence-based and dynamic (FO-001, Section 2).
- Score inputs are documented and reviewed for bias (FO-004).
- Members can see the categories that inform their score, not raw weights, to prevent gaming.
- The Founder approves any change to Trust Score logic.
- Trust Score never overrides a human safety decision.

## 8. Safety Escalation

- Any threat to life, violence, or illegal activity escalates immediately to the Founder and, where required, to law enforcement.
- AI systems escalate safety risks to humans (FO-004, AI Escalation Rules).
- Escalations are documented and reviewed in the Weekly Founder Review (FO-005).

## 9. Crisis Communication

In a community or safety crisis, Nmood communicates per FO-008:

- Members first, honestly and quickly.
- No spin, no minimization.
- Clear guidance on what to do and what Nmood is doing.
- A single, authoritative source of truth.

## 10. Healthy Community Metrics

A healthy community is measured by:

- Experience completion rate.
- Member retention within the community.
- Report rate and resolution quality.
- Trust score distribution.
- Diversity of participation.
- Absence of harm signals.

A community is not healthy because it is large; it is healthy because it is safe, trusted, and active in the real world.
