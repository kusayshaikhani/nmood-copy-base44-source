## Purpose

To protect members, preserve trust, and ensure business continuity during any crisis.

## Scope

This Manual governs response to security incidents, data breaches, service outages, AI failures, reputation events, media response, legal escalation, disaster recovery, business continuity, and emergency contacts.

## Crisis Operating Principles

Across every crisis type:

1. **Members first.** Their safety and data before reputation or revenue.
2. **Truth, fast.** Say what is known, what is not known, and what is being done.
3. **Preserve evidence.** Logs, audit trails, and system state are retained.
4. **Founder-led.** The Founder owns the response; delegation is explicit.
5. **Single voice.** One authoritative channel; no speculation.

## 1. Security Incidents

- Detect, contain, assess, notify, recover.
- Severity is classified by member impact, not system impact alone.
- Critical incidents escalate immediately to the Founder (FO-005).
- All actions logged in SecurityEvent and AuditLog entities.

## 2. Data Breaches

- Contain the breach and secure affected systems.
- Identify the scope of affected member data.
- Notify affected members honestly and promptly, per applicable law.
- Notify regulators where legally required.
- Provide affected members clear protective guidance.
- Post-incident review and remediation are mandatory.

## 3. Service Outages

- Communicate status within a defined SLA.
- Prioritize restoration of safety-critical features first.
- Provide honest estimates, never false reassurance.
- Conduct a post-incident review and publish a summary.

## 4. AI Failures

- Halt the affected AI system immediately (FO-004).
- Assess member harm and reverse automated decisions where possible.
- Escalate to human review for all affected interactions.
- Log the failure in the AI audit record.
- Restrict the system until the root cause is corrected and re-certified (FO-004, AI Release Policy).

## 5. Reputation Management

- Reputation is a function of trust, not messaging.
- Respond with facts and action, not spin.
- Never minimize harm to protect image.
- Correct misinformation promptly and calmly.
- The long-term reputation is rebuilt by behavior, not by press releases.

## 6. Media Response

- All media response is coordinated by the Founder or designated spokesperson (FO-002).
- Statements are factual, brief, and honest.
- No speculation. No off-record contradictions of this Manual.
- Member data is never disclosed to media without legal basis and consent.

## 7. Legal Escalation

- Engage legal counsel immediately for incidents with legal exposure.
- Preserve all relevant evidence and communications.
- Cooperate with law enforcement where required and lawful.
- Never obstruct an investigation.
- The Founder approves any public legal statement.

## 8. Disaster Recovery

- Nmood maintains backups and recovery procedures per its data retention and backup policy.
- Recovery objectives are defined for critical systems.
- Recovery is tested on a regular cadence.
- Recovery prioritizes member safety and data integrity.

## 9. Business Continuity

- Critical functions are identified and have continuity plans.
- The company can operate in a degraded mode during a major incident.
- Key roles have documented backups.
- Continuity is reviewed annually (FO-005, Quarterly Review).

## 10. Emergency Contacts Structure

A defined escalation structure is maintained and kept current:

- **Founder** — ultimate authority in any crisis.
- **Security lead** — security incidents, breaches.
- **Safety lead** — member safety, threats, illegal activity.
- **AI governance owner** — AI failures (FO-004).
- **Legal counsel** — legal escalation.
- **Communications lead** — media and member communication.

Specific names and contact methods are maintained in a secure, access-controlled directory, reviewed monthly, and available to the Founder and designated leads at all times.
