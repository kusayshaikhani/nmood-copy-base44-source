## Purpose

To give the Founder a permanent operating rhythm that protects the mission (FO-001), sustains trust, and resists short-term pressure across years and decades.

## Scope

This document governs the Founder's responsibilities, decision frameworks, reviews, and crisis leadership. It does not delegate Founder authority; it structures how that authority is exercised.

## 1. Founder Responsibilities

The Founder is the permanent guardian of:

- The mission and vision (FO-001).
- The Founder Documentation Suite.
- The trust placed in Nmood by members.
- The integrity of the AI Constitution (FO-004).
- The safety and privacy of members.
- The long-term direction of the company (FO-003).

The Founder delegates execution but never the authority to revise this Suite or weaken a Permanent Rule (FO-001, Section 18).

## 2. Decision Framework

Every Founder decision is tested against the FO-001 Decision Framework:

> "Does this help people build meaningful human relationships?"

- **Yes** → proceed through review.
- **No** → do not proceed.
- **Unclear** → defer until clear.

Secondary test: "Does this protect or erode trust?"

A decision that grows the company while eroding trust is refused.

## 3. Product Decision Matrix

| Impact | Trust / Safety Impact | Approval Required |
|--------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Low | None | Product owner |
| Medium | None | Product lead + Founder review |
| Medium | Any | Founder approval |
| High | Any | Founder approval + audit log |
| Crisis | Any | Founder + Crisis Manual (FO-008) |

No product decision that touches safety, privacy, AI autonomy, or a Permanent Rule ships without Founder approval.

## 4. Hiring Philosophy

- Hire for values first, capability second (FO-001, Core Values).
- Hire people who are warmer, calmer, and more honest than the role requires.
- Never hire for obedience over judgment.
- Never hire someone who would weaken the trust of existing members.
- Reference checks weight trust and integrity above technical skill.

## 5. Leadership Principles

- **Model the values** — the team copies the Founder, not the handbook.
- **Decide slowly when possible, fast when needed.**
- **Communicate the why** before the what.
- **Protect the team from short-term pressure** so they can serve the member.
- **Own failures publicly; share credit widely.**

## 6. Meeting Principles

- Every meeting has a purpose, an agenda, and an outcome.
- No meeting without a decision or a relationship being built.
- Default to async; meet to decide or connect, not to inform.
- End on time, every time.
- One decision per meeting is a successful meeting.

## 7. Weekly Founder Review

A standing weekly review covering:

- Trust and safety incidents (open and resolved).
- AI system health and escalations (FO-004).
- Product release status (FO-003).
- Member feedback themes.
- Key metric movement (MRWC and supporting metrics, FO-003, Section 13).
- Founder decisions made and pending.

## 8. Monthly Review

A deeper monthly review covering:

- Progress against the roadmap (FO-003).
- Community health (FO-007).
- Brand consistency (FO-002).
- Financial and operational health.
- People and culture (FO-009).
- Risk register review (FO-010).

## 9. Quarterly Review

A strategic quarterly review covering:

- Roadmap re-baselining (FO-003).
- Constitution and Manual review (FO-001, FO-004).
- Audit log review for Founder Overrides.
- Governance review of this Suite.
- 3-year and 5-year vision check (FO-003).

## 10. KPI Review

The Founder reviews the North Star Metric — Meaningful Real-World Connections (MRWC) — and the supporting metrics defined in FO-003, Section 13. KPIs that reward attention over connection are explicitly excluded from the Founder's review.

## 11. Crisis Leadership

In a crisis, the Founder operates per FO-008 (Crisis Management Manual). The Founder's role is to:

- Protect members first.
- Communicate honestly and quickly.
- Preserve evidence and audit trails.
- Make and own the hard calls.
- Resist pressure to prioritize reputation over safety.

## 12. Decision Escalation

Escalation paths:

- **Product → Product lead → Founder.**
- **Safety → Safety lead → Founder (immediate).**
- **AI → AI governance owner → Founder (FO-004).**
- **Legal → Legal counsel → Founder.**
- **Crisis → Crisis Manual → Founder (FO-008).**

No one is permitted to escalate around the Founder for a Permanent Rule decision; only the Founder can approve those.

## 13. Long-Term Thinking

The Founder measures Nmood in decades, not quarters (FO-001, Section 16). Where a short-term decision and the long-term vision conflict, the long term wins — unless the short-term decision is required to keep members safe.
