## Purpose

Objectives and Key Results align the company behind outcomes, not activity. Each function has one Objective and 3–5 measurable Key Results per quarter. OKRs are scored 0.0–1.0 at quarter end. The Founder reviews OKR progress weekly (EOS-005) and scores them at the Quarterly Review.

---

## Scoring

- **0.0–0.3** Red — missed
- **0.4–0.6** Amber — partial
- **0.7–1.0** Green — achieved or exceeded
- **Target:** Average 0.7 company-wide. Consistently hitting 1.0 means targets are too easy; consistently 0.4 means they're unrealistic or execution is failing.

## OKR Lifecycle

1. **Draft (week -2 of quarter):** Founder drafts company OKRs; leads draft function OKRs.
2. **Align (week -1):** Founder reviews; KR targets challenged; dependencies resolved.
3. **Commit (week 0):** OKRs locked and published.
4. **Track (weekly):** Progress reviewed in Weekly Review (EOS-005).
5. **Score (week +13):** Founder scores each KR 0.0–1.0 at Quarterly Review.
6. **Learn:** Scored OKRs feed the next quarter's draft and the Annual Review.

---

## Rules

- OKRs are outcomes, not tasks. "Ship feature X" is a task; "Increase activation to 60%" is an outcome.
- A KR is measurable. If you can't measure it, it's not a KR.
- Maximum 5 KRs per objective. Focus is the point.
- OKRs are not compensation. They guide effort, not pay.
- No sandbagging. A 1.0 on every KR means the targets were too easy.
