# EXEC-005 — Founder Review System

**Suite:** Nmood Executive Operating System  
**ID:** EOS-005  
**Status:** Permanent operating system  
**Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Nmood Founder  
**Reference:** FO-005 (Founder Playbook §7)

---

## Purpose

The Review System is the Founder's rhythm of reflection. It converts raw metrics and events into lessons, decisions, and action. Every review has a template, a cadence, and an archive. No review is skipped; no review is ceremonial.

---

## Review Principles

1. **Honest before encouraging.** A red metric is named red.
2. **Decisions are recorded.** A meeting without a logged decision didn't happen.
3. **Action items have owners and dates.** An item without an owner is a wish.
4. **Lessons are written.** The same mistake should not be explained twice.
5. **The mission is checked.** Every review asks: are we helping people build meaningful relationships?

## 1. Daily Review

**Cadence:** Every morning, 15 minutes, with the Daily Brief (EOS-001).  
**Participants:** Founder (solo).

**Template:**
```
DAILY REVIEW — [date]

KPIs (from Daily Brief)
  Red: [list]
  Amber: [list]
  Green: [list]

Wins (yesterday)
  1. [win]
  2. [win]

Problems
  1. [problem] — owner: [name]

Risks (active)
  [risk] — severity — action

Lessons
  [one sentence]

Decisions
  [decision] — rationale

Action Items
  [ ] [item] — owner — due: [date]

Founder Priority (today)
  1. [one thing]
```

**Archive:** Stored daily; feeds the Weekly Review.

---

## Archive & Continuity

- Every review is stored in the operations archive with date, attendees, and decisions.
- The Founder reviews the prior period's review before starting the next — continuity is the point.
- Decisions are cross-referenced in the Decision Log (EOS-010) and traceable from review to implementation.
- No review is deleted or rewritten; the archive is the honest record.
