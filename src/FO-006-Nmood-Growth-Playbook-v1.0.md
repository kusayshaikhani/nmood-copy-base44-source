## Purpose

To define how Nmood grows in a way that strengthens trust and real-world connection — the only kind of growth the company accepts.

## Scope

This document governs growth philosophy, marketing, referrals, community growth, ambassador and university programs, corporate partnerships, launch strategy, international expansion, brand partnerships, and growth KPIs.

## 1. Growth Philosophy

Growth through:

- Trust.
- Referrals.
- Great experiences.
- Word of mouth.
- Community.

Never through (FO-001, Section 12):

- Manipulation.
- Spam.
- Dark patterns.
- Addictive mechanics.

Growth is a byproduct of trust. A member who has a meaningful experience invites the next member. That is the only growth loop Nmood engineers.

## 2. Marketing Principles

- **Truthful.** Never over-promise. Never imply romance (FO-001, Section 6).
- **Calm.** No urgency, no FOMO, no countdown pressure.
- **Human.** Real member stories (with consent) over actors.
- **Localized.** Every campaign localized through the Nmood Localization Service (FO-001, Section 4).
- **Brand-aligned.** Every asset reviewed against FO-002.

## 3. Referral Strategy

- Members invite members after a real, positive experience.
- Referrals reward both sides with trust signals, not cash gimmicks.
- Referral incentives never pressure members to invite people they do not genuinely recommend.
- Quality of connection matters more than volume of invites.

## 4. Community Growth

Communities grow by becoming genuinely valuable to their members.

- Empower trusted hosts and circle leads (FO-007).
- Provide tools, not vanity metrics.
- Never pay communities for growth; support them for health.
- A healthy community grows itself.

## 5. Ambassador Program

Ambassadors are trusted members who represent Nmood in a city, campus, or community.

- Selected for alignment with FO-001 values, not following.
- Empowered to host and welcome new members.
- Compensated fairly and transparently.
- Held to the same Community Principles as every member (FO-001, Section 8).

## 6. University Program

A targeted program to help students build real social connections.

- Partner with student organizations, not just administrations.
- Privacy-first: no data shared with institutions beyond consent.
- Focus on belonging, not recruitment.
- Measured by MRWC within the student population, not installs.

## 7. Corporate Partnerships

Partnerships that help employees connect in real life.

- Partners selected for alignment with FO-001, not only budget.
- No employer access to employee member data.
- Members retain full control of their Nmood identity.
- Partnership terms reviewed against FO-001 Permanent Rules.

## 8. Launch Strategy

- Launch in one city deeply before expanding widely.
- Earn density of experiences and trust before scale.
- Launch is a trust exercise, not a marketing exercise.
- Localized launch in the local language from day one (FO-001, Section 4).

## 9. International Expansion

- Expand only where Nmood can operate lawfully and safely (FO-001, Section 18).
- Localize fully — language, culture, payment, and law — before launch.
- Respect the religion, culture, and norms of each market (FO-001, Section 8).
- Never enter a market where Nmood cannot protect member privacy.

## 10. Brand Partnerships

- Partners must pass the same values test as employees (FO-009).
- Co-branding never compromises Nmood's visual or voice standards (FO-002).
- No partner may use Nmood to access member data outside explicit consent.
- Partnerships that conflict with FO-001 are refused, regardless of value.

## 11. Success KPIs

**Primary:** Meaningful Real-World Connections (MRWC) growth (FO-003).

**Trust:** referral-driven signup share, member Net Promoter, trust score trend.

**Health:** experience completion rate, circle retention, 90-day member retention.

**Avoided as primary:** raw installs, daily active users as an end, paid acquisition efficiency above trust quality.
