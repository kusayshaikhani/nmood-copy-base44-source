## Consistency Standards

Every document in this Suite:

- Uses one consistent writing style — executive, plain, honest.
- Includes: Title block, Version History, Purpose, Scope, Table of Contents, body sections, and revision control.
- References FO-001 where appropriate and avoids duplication by cross-referencing.
- Is suitable for future employees, executives, advisors, investors, auditors, and board members.
- Is governed by the Founder approval and revision process defined in FO-001.

---

## Suite Governance

- This Suite may only be revised by Founder approval (FO-001, Document Governance).
- Every revision records Version, Date, Reason, and Approval in the document's Version History.
- No revision may weaken a Permanent Rule (FO-001, Section 18) without explicit, recorded Founder approval.
- The Suite prevails in any conflict with a short-term business objective, unless formally revised.
