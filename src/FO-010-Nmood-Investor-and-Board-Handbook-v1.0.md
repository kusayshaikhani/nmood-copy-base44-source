## 1. Company Overview

Nmood is an AI-powered Human Connection Platform that helps people build meaningful real-world relationships through shared interests, trusted communities, and real experiences. Nmood is built to be the most trusted platform of its kind — measured in decades, not quarters.

## 2. Mission

To help people build meaningful real-world relationships through AI-powered recommendations, shared interests, trusted communities, and experiences — and to use technology to encourage people to spend less time on screens and more time living (FO-001, Section 1).

## 3. Vision

To become the world's most trusted AI-powered Human Connection Platform (FO-001, Section 2; FO-003, Section 1).

## 4. Market Opportunity

A global population increasingly connected digitally but disconnected personally. Existing platforms optimize for attention, not connection. Nmood addresses the unmet need for trust-first, real-world human connection — a market defined less by category and more by the gap between what people have (screen time) and what people want (real relationships).

## 5. Product Strategy

Build the trusted core first (Release 1.0), deepen trust and inclusion (Release 1.1), then open as a governed platform (Release 2.0). The North Star Metric is Meaningful Real-World Connections (MRWC), not engagement (FO-003).

## 6. AI Strategy

AI assists humans; it does not replace, manipulate, or rank them. AI at Nmood is governed by the AI Constitution (FO-004) — responsible, explainable, human-overseen, and privacy-first. AI is a durable competitive advantage only because it is trusted.

## 7. Growth Strategy

Grow through trust, referrals, great experiences, word of mouth, and community — never through manipulation, spam, dark patterns, or addictive mechanics (FO-001, Section 12; FO-006). Expansion is city-by-city and language-by-language, with full localization before launch.

## 8. Governance

- The Founder holds final authority over the Founder Documentation Suite (FO-001, Governance).
- The board advises; the Founder decides on mission, values, and Permanent Rules.
- AI is governed by FO-004.
- Community and trust are governed by FO-007.
- Crises are governed by FO-008.
- Culture and people are governed by FO-009.

## 9. Risk Management

Key risks and mitigations:

- **Trust erosion** → Safety and Trust Playbook (FO-007), transparent communication.
- **AI harm** → AI Constitution (FO-004), audit policy, human oversight.
- **Data breach** → Crisis Manual (FO-008), secure architecture, retention policy.
- **Regulatory change** → legal monitoring, market-by-market compliance (FO-001, Section 18).
- **Growth pressure** → Permanent Rules, North Star Metric, Founder authority.
- **People risk** → Culture Handbook (FO-009), hiring standards, succession.

Risk is reviewed in the Founder's Quarterly Review (FO-005).

## 10. Board Principles

- The board exists to steward the mission, not to maximize short-term return.
- Board members are selected for alignment with FO-001 values.
- The board advises on strategy, risk, and governance; the Founder decides on mission and Permanent Rules.
- Board members are bound by the same confidentiality and ethical standards as employees (FO-009).
- No board decision may weaken a Permanent Rule without Founder approval (FO-001, Section 18).

## 11. Investor Communication Standards

- Honest, always. Good news and bad news are shared with equal candor.
- No hype. No vanity metrics. The company reports MRWC and trust metrics, not engagement theater (FO-003, Section 13).
- Forward statements are clearly labeled as expectations, not promises.
- Material events are communicated promptly per FO-008 where they constitute a crisis.
- Investor materials are consistent with the Brand Bible (FO-002) — calm, clear, truthful.

## 12. Reporting Cadence

- **Monthly:** operational and metric summary.
- **Quarterly:** strategic review, roadmap progress, risk register, financial summary.
- **Annually:** full Suite review, audit, long-term vision check, and Founder letter.

Reports are shared with the board and investors on a defined, reliable cadence.

## 13. Long-Term Objectives

- **3 years:** trusted category leader in launch markets, profitable in primary market, 12+ languages, public recognition for safety and privacy (FO-003, Section 9).
- **5 years:** one of the world's most trusted platforms for genuine human connection, multi-continent, responsible AI reference (FO-003, Section 10).
- **Decades:** a durable institution that outlasts its founders and remains a trusted place for human connection for generations (FO-001, Section 16).
