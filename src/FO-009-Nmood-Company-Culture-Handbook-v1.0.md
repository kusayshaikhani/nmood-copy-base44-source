## 1. Company Culture

Nmood's culture is the lived form of FO-001. We are warm, calm, honest, and human. We put members first, trust before growth, and safety before engagement. We measure ourselves in decades, not quarters.

## 2. Hiring Standards

- Hire for values first, capability second (FO-005).
- Never hire someone who would weaken member trust.
- Reference checks weight integrity above skill.
- Diverse candidate slates are the default, not the exception.
- Every hire is approved against these standards.

## 3. Employee Values

Every Nmood employee is expected to:

- Put members first.
- Build for trust, safety, and privacy.
- Speak honestly, including upward.
- Own mistakes and correct them.
- Respect every colleague and every member equally.
- Choose the long term over the easy term.

## 4. Communication Principles

- **Clear.** Plain language, one idea at a time.
- **Honest.** Say what is true, including when it is uncomfortable.
- **Kind.** Assume the best in colleagues.
- **Async by default.** Meet to decide or connect, not to inform.
- **Documented.** Decisions are written so they outlast the people who made them.

## 5. Diversity & Inclusion

- Nmood serves a global, multi-cultural community; the team must reflect it.
- Inclusion is a standard, not an initiative.
- Discrimination of any kind is incompatible with employment at Nmood (FO-001, Section 8).
- Accessibility is part of inclusion — in product and in workplace (FO-001, Section 9).

## 6. Remote Work

- Remote and distributed work is supported where the role allows.
- Outcomes matter more than hours or location.
- Async communication is respected; synchronous time is protected.
- Team members are trusted and accountable.

## 7. Leadership Expectations

- Model the values (FO-005).
- Protect the team from short-term pressure.
- Decide clearly and communicate the why.
- Develop successors; no role should depend on one person indefinitely.
- Be accountable to the Founder and to the members.

## 8. Performance Philosophy

- Performance is measured by contribution to the mission and trust, not by visibility or hours.
- Feedback is frequent, specific, and kind.
- Underperformance is addressed honestly and early.
- Excellence is recognized; politics are not.

## 9. Learning Culture

- Mistakes are learning, not failure — when owned and corrected.
- Continuous learning is supported and expected.
- The team learns from members, from data, and from each other.
- This Suite is living; the team is expected to challenge and improve it through the governance process (FO-001, Review Process).

## 10. Ethical Conduct

- Every employee follows FO-001 and this Handbook.
- Conflicts of interest are disclosed.
- Member data is never misused.
- No one is asked to act against their conscience or the law.
- Ethical concerns can be raised without fear of retaliation.
