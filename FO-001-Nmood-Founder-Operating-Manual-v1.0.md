## Executive Summary

Nmood is an AI-powered Human Connection Platform built to help people form meaningful real-world relationships through shared interests, trusted communities, and real experiences. This manual is the permanent operating doctrine of the company. It exists to protect the mission across decades, leadership transitions, funding rounds, and market pressure. It defines what Nmood is, what it refuses to be, and the non-negotiable principles that govern every product, AI, community, safety, privacy, and growth decision.

The central rule of this manual is simple: **every product decision must answer the question — "Does this help people build meaningful human relationships?"** If the answer is no, the feature should not be built. This manual may only be revised by Founder approval, and every revision is recorded in the Version History.

## Purpose

This manual exists to:

1. **Protect the mission** of Nmood across time, personnel, and market conditions.
2. **Provide a permanent reference** for employees, executives, investors, advisors, and board members on how Nmood operates.
3. **Establish non-negotiable principles** that cannot be overridden by short-term commercial pressure.
4. **Define governance** for how the company and its AI systems are managed.
5. **Create accountability** for leadership, staff, and systems.

This manual is not advisory. Where a conflict exists between this manual and a short-term business objective, **this manual prevails** unless formally revised by the Founder.

---

## Scope

This manual applies to:

- The Nmood product, platform, and all software systems.
- All employees, contractors, interns, and advisors.
- All partners, vendors, and third-party service providers acting on behalf of Nmood.
- All moderators and community management staff.
- All AI systems, models, and automated decision-making processes operated by Nmood.
- The Founder, executives, and all leadership.

This manual covers:

- Mission, vision, values, and principles.
- Product, AI, community, safety, and privacy doctrine.
- Growth, release, and decision frameworks.
- Company culture and long-term vision.
- Document governance and revision process.

This manual does **not** replace employment contracts, local labor law, financial regulation, or applicable national law. Where this manual and the law conflict, the law prevails; where the law is silent, this manual governs.

## Guiding Principles

1. **The mission is the product.** Nmood exists to serve human connection, not to extract attention.
2. **Trust is the only durable currency.** It is earned slowly and lost quickly.
3. **Safety outweighs growth. Engagement never outweighs trust.**
4. **Real-world outcomes are the success metric.** Screens are a means, not the end.
5. **AI assists humans. It does not replace, manipulate, or override them.**
6. **Privacy is a core feature, not a setting.**
7. **The Founder is the permanent guardian of this manual.**

## Governance

### Founder Authority

The Founder holds final authority over:

- This manual and all revisions to it.
- The mission, vision, and permanent principles of Nmood.
- Any deviation from a Permanent Rule.
- The appointment of executive leadership.
- The authorization of Founder Overrides.

### Operational Delegation

The Founder may delegate day-to-day operations to executives, employees, contractors, and moderators. Delegation does not transfer the authority to revise this manual or to override a Permanent Rule.

### AI Governance

All AI systems operate under the AI Principles (Section 7). The Founder, supported by the Mission Control platform, retains oversight of all AI-driven decisions. No AI system may act autonomously in a way that violates the Safety Principles (Section 9) or Privacy Principles (Section 10).

### Audit and Accountability

Every Founder Override, privileged administrative action, and material AI decision is recorded in an immutable audit log. Audit records are retained per the Nmood data retention policy and are available for Founder review at any time.

---

## Review Process

This manual is reviewed:

- **Annually**, on the anniversary of its effective date.
- **On demand**, whenever the Founder deems a revision necessary.
- **Upon a material change** to Nmood's mission, product, legal environment, or market.

### Revision Procedure

1. A proposed revision is drafted and circulated to the Founder.
2. The Founder reviews the proposed revision.
3. If approved, the revision is incorporated and a new row is added to the Version History with:
   - **Version**
   - **Date**
   - **Reason**
   - **Approval**
4. No revision takes effect until it is recorded in the Version History and approved by the Founder.

A revision may **add** to this manual or **clarify** it, but no revision may **weaken** a Permanent Rule without explicit Founder approval recorded in the Version History.

## 1. Mission

Nmood exists to help people build meaningful real-world relationships through AI-powered recommendations, shared interests, trusted communities, and experiences.

Technology should encourage people to spend less time on screens and more time living.

The mission is not a marketing statement. It is the test against which every product, feature, partnership, and growth initiative is measured.

---

## 2. Vision

Become the world's most trusted AI-powered Human Connection Platform.

Trust is the operative word. Nmood does not aspire to be the largest platform. It aspires to be the most trusted. Size, where it comes, must be a consequence of trust — never a substitute for it.

---

## 3. Core Values

These values are permanent. They govern behavior, hiring, partnerships, and product decisions.

- **Trust** — We earn it, protect it, and never trade it.
- **Respect** — For every human, equally and without exception.
- **Integrity** — We do what is right, especially when it is costly.
- **Inclusivity** — We welcome people of every background, culture, and identity.
- **Human Connection** — Real relationships are the product, not the byproduct.
- **Safety** — We protect members before we grow.
- **Transparency** — We are honest with members, staff, and partners.
- **Accountability** — We own our decisions and their consequences.
- **Innovation** — We improve continuously, in service of the mission.
- **Simplicity** — We remove what does not help people connect.

---

## 4. Product Principles

1. **Human First** — People come before metrics, algorithms, and revenue.
2. **Trust before Growth** — We do not grow what we cannot trust.
3. **Safety before Engagement** — We will reduce engagement before we reduce safety.
4. **Privacy by Design** — Privacy is built in, not bolted on.
5. **AI assists humans — not replaces them.** — AI recommends; humans decide.
6. **Real-world experiences over endless scrolling.** — The screen is a doorway, not a destination.
7. **Quality over quantity.** — Fewer, better connections beat many shallow ones.

These principles are enforced at the design stage. A feature that violates any of them is not shipped.

---

## 5. What Nmood Is

Nmood is an **AI Human Connection Platform**. Specifically:

- **An AI Human Connection Platform** — using AI to recommend meaningful relationships, not to maximize attention.
- **An Experience Platform** — helping people discover, join, and host real-world activities.
- **A Community Platform** — building trusted groups around shared interests, locations, and values.
- **A Trust Platform** — verifying, scoring, and protecting the integrity of every member and organizer.

---

## 6. What Nmood Is NOT

Nmood is deliberately not any of the following. Building toward these is a violation of this manual.

- **Not a Dating App** — Nmood connects people for friendship, community, and shared experience — not romantic matching.
- **Not Social Media** — Nmood does not reward follower counts, viral content, or public performance.
- **Not a Follower Economy** — There are no follower counts to optimize. Status is earned through trust, not audience size.
- **Not a Swipe Platform** — People are not profiles to be accepted or dismissed in seconds.
- **Not a Popularity Contest** — Nmood does not rank humans by appeal. Every member is equal.

---

## 7. AI Principles

Nmood uses AI as a tool to serve human connection. AI at Nmood is governed by these permanent rules:

- **Responsible AI** — Every AI system is accountable for its outputs.
- **Human Oversight** — A human can review, override, or halt any AI decision.
- **Transparency** — Members are told when AI is used and for what.
- **Explainability** — AI recommendations can be explained in plain language.
- **Bias Reduction** — We actively test for and reduce bias in recommendations.
- **User Control** — Members can adjust, refuse, or reset AI-driven personalization.
- **Privacy** — AI never uses member data beyond what the member has consented to.
- **Ethical Recommendations** — AI recommends what helps people connect, not what keeps them scrolling.

No AI system at Nmood may be designed to manipulate a member's attention, emotion, or behavior against the member's interest.

## 9. Safety Principles

Nmood's safety philosophy is permanent and non-negotiable.

- **Safety always outweighs growth.** A growth initiative that compromises safety is rejected.
- **Trust always outweighs engagement.** An engagement feature that erodes trust is rejected.
- **Safety is proactive, not reactive.** We design for safety before harm occurs.
- **Safety is visible.** Members can see, understand, and use the tools that protect them.
- **Safety is enforced consistently.** Rules apply equally to every member, regardless of status.

---

## 10. Privacy Principles

- **Privacy by Design.** Every feature is built to collect the minimum data necessary.
- **Minimal Data Collection.** We collect what we need to serve the member — nothing more.
- **Transparency.** Members know what we collect, why, and with whom it is shared.
- **User Control.** Members can access, correct, export, and delete their data.
- **Secure Architecture.** Data is protected by strong, current security controls and is never sold.

Privacy is a **core feature** of Nmood. It is never deprecated for convenience or growth.

## 11. Founder Principles

- **Protect the mission** above personal, financial, and market pressure.
- **Think long-term** — in decades, not quarters.
- **Lead ethically** — setting the standard the rest of the company is asked to meet.
- **Make user-first decisions** — even when they are commercially uncomfortable.

- **Improve continuously** — revising this manual and the platform in service of the mission.

The Founder is the permanent guardian of this manual and of the trust placed in Nmood.

## 12. Growth Philosophy

### Growth Through

- **Trust.**
- **Referrals.**
- **Great experiences.**
- **Word of mouth.**
- **Community.**

### Never Through

- **Manipulation.**
- **Spam.**
- **Dark patterns.**
- **Addictive mechanics.**

Growth that depends on deception or compulsion is not growth — it is debt. Nmood refuses to incur it.

---

## 13. Decision Framework

Every product decision at Nmood must answer one question:

> **"Does this help people build meaningful human relationships?"**

- If the answer is **yes**, the decision may proceed through normal product, safety, and privacy review.
- If the answer is **no**, the feature should not be built.
- If the answer is **unclear**, the decision is deferred until it can be answered clearly.

This framework overrides commercial pressure, competitive pressure, and growth targets. It is the single test to which all product decisions are subject.

---

## 14. Release Philosophy

Every release of Nmood must improve at least one of:

- **Security.**
- **Performance.**
- **Accessibility.**
- **User Experience.**
- **Trust.**
- **Safety.**
- **Reliability.**

A release that improves none of these is not shipped. A release that degrades any of them is not shipped until the degradation is corrected.

## 15. Company Culture

Nmood's culture is an extension of this manual. The same standards apply to everyone who works on or for the platform.

### Employees
- Put members first.
- Build for trust, safety, and privacy.
- Speak honestly, including upward.
- Own mistakes and correct them.

### Contractors
- Operate under the same principles as employees.
- Are accountable for the work they deliver and the data they access.

### Partners
- Are selected for alignment with Nmood's values, not only capability or price.
- Are terminated when their conduct conflicts with this manual.

### Moderators
- Enforce the Community Principles consistently and fairly.
- Are protected, trained, and supported.
- Never let growth, status, or commercial concern override a safety decision.

### AI Systems
- Operate under the AI Principles (Section 7).
- Are monitored for bias, error, and harm.
- Are corrected or retired when they fail the Decision Framework (Section 13).

### Leadership
- Models the values in this manual.
- Protects the mission above short-term results.
- Is accountable to the Founder and, through the Founder, to the members.

## 16. Long-Term Vision

Nmood's long-term objective is to build **one of the world's most trusted platforms for genuine human connection.**

This vision is measured in **decades, not quarters.** Nmood accepts slower growth, smaller metrics, and harder decisions in exchange for durability and trust. The company is built to outlast its founders, its current leadership, and its current market — to remain a trusted place for human connection for generations.

## 18. Permanent Product Rules

The following rules are **immutable**. They cannot be removed or weakened without Founder approval, recorded in the Version History.

1. **Respect every human equally.** No member, community, or partner is above another.
2. **Respect the laws of every country where Nmood operates.** Where local law conflicts with this manual, the law prevails; Nmood does not operate in a way that breaks it.
3. **Respect all religions and beliefs.** Nmood is neutral and inclusive on matters of faith.
4. **AI must never manipulate users.** AI recommends; it never deceives, coerces, or exploits.
5. **Founder Overrides must always be audited.** No privileged action is taken without an immutable audit record.
6. **Privacy is a core feature.** It is never deprecated, sold, or traded.
7. **Trust cannot be compromised for growth.** Growth that erodes trust is refused.
