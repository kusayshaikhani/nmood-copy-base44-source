Delete your Nmood account

Nmood is operated by Lazy Panda FZE LLC. You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data in the app or by email. You do not need to sign in to use the email request below.

Request deletion by email

Email support@nmood.app with the subject Nmood account deletion request. Include the email address associated with your Nmood account. If possible, send the request from that address. If you no longer have access to it, explain this so Support can help verify ownership.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes or payment-card details. Support verifies account ownership before acting on a request.

Email an account-deletion request

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open Nmood, go to Settings, and find Delete Account in the account/privacy controls.
  2. Complete the requested reauthentication with your linked sign-in method.
  3. Review the deletion notice, type DELETE, and confirm.

Confirmed new requests start permanent deletion immediately. Account access is blocked and you are signed out. There is no recovery period for new requests. If an external service delays erasure, access remains blocked while deletion is retried.

Data deleted

Your account and associated personal data are erased or anonymized, except for the limited records described below. This includes:

  • Account identifiers, email, phone number and profile information, including name, bio, birth date, gender and nationality.
  • Profile photos, gallery photos and other owned media.
  • Interests, languages, lifestyle details, location and privacy preferences.
  • Personalization settings and analytics consent preferences.

Hosted-media invalidation can take up to 60 seconds after server erasure. Copies already downloaded by other people or saved in browser caches cannot be recalled. A processing status does not mean every erasure step is finished.

Limited retention exceptions

Only records needed for safety, fraud prevention, legal compliance or dispute handling are retained, with personal data minimized or anonymized where possible.

Payment and tax recordsOnly for the legally required period and/or the payment provider’s applicable retention period. Nmood does not store payment-card details.

These retained records do not allow account recovery. Requests accepted under an earlier recovery policy retain their recorded terms.

Subscriptions and sign-in providers

Deleting Nmood does not replace cancellation of a store subscription. Manage any active subscription in Google Play or the Apple App Store. You can also remove Nmood’s access through your Google or Apple account settings.

Questions or deletion requests: support@nmood.app.
